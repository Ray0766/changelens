# Sample files

The demo PDFs here (and the test PDFs in `eval/cases/` and `test/fixtures/`) are **synthetic**. Companies, people, prices and terms are invented. They were generated with headless Chromium by `scripts/make-samples.mjs` and `eval/make-cases.mjs`; `test/fixtures/agreement-*.pdf` was generated with LibreOffice.

Fonts embedded in these PDFs, under their own licences:
